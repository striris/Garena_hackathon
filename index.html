    CINDERFALL

    A world that refuses to sit still.

    Two peoples fight over a ring of islands.
    Underneath them, the mountain that made those islands is still working — and what it cannot stand is a quiet map.

    ASHFARERS · you SALTKIN · the rival CINDER · the world itself
    THE WORLD

    A mountain that makes land, and takes it back

    Far out in a warm sea there is a ring of islands. They were not always there. They were made, one by one, by a mountain that still lives under the water. The people who live there call it Cinder.

    Cinder is still working. It pushes new islands up out of the sea. It pulls old ones back down. Ash from its mouth falls on the water and turns to the richest soil anyone has ever farmed. Land here is young, and it is generous, and it does not stay.

    Two peoples share the ring. The Ashfarers came first and learned to farm the ash fields. The Saltkin came later, by sea, and took the high ground. Neither of them is evil. The problem is simpler than that: there is not enough steady ground for both, and every season there is less.

    Cinder does not care who wins. What Cinder cannot stand is stillness — a quiet sea, a settled border, two peoples who have stopped moving. So when the fighting stops, the mountain does something about it.

    ★
    THE BEACON. Somewhere on the ring a fire burns on a stone tower. Nobody lit it. Holding it means good harvests and a claim to the ring — but the Beacon is Cinder's, and Cinder moves it. Settle into a quiet stalemate and one night the fire goes out and relights in open ground, exactly between you. That is how the mountain starts a war without touching anybody.
    HOW TO PLAY

    Six numbers a square, and one comparison

    1SPEND YOUR INCOME

    Each turn you earn the fertility of every square you hold and can still reach. Spend it all — nothing carries over.

    • EXPANDclaim empty land next to yours, at strength 12
    • FORTIFYadd 2 strength to a square you hold1
    • RAIDattack an enemy square next to yours3
    2COMBAT IS ONE COMPARISON
    ATTACKstrength of the square you attack from + 3
    DEFENCEstrength of the target + its height

    Higher attack takes the square at strength 1, and the attacking square loses 2. Otherwise the attack fails and the defender loses 1.

    No dice, no unit types. All of the surprise in this game comes from the mountain, not from luck here.

    3SUPPLY

    A square only counts if you can trace a path of your own land back to your capital. Cut-off squares earn nothing and lose 1 strength a turn until they are empty.

    Which is why splitting a territory in half hurts more than destroying part of it.

    4THE MOUNTAIN ACTS

    Every 3 turns Cinder reads the match and does one thing to the world: an eruption, a sinking coast, a new island, the fire moving, the high ground going cold.

    It always warns you one turn early, and it always says what you did to deserve it. You are never surprised by a disaster you had no chance to prepare for.

    5WINNING

    Reach 10 Beacon Points — one for each turn you end holding the Beacon — or hold the most land after 25 turns.

    6CONTROLS

    Pick a tool with 1 2 3, then click the map. Click a queued order to remove it, or right-click the square. ⏎ ends the turn.

    Both sides give orders at the same time, in secret, and everything resolves together.
