    1 / 3 · READ THE RING

    Two fronts. Two cross-bridges. One enemy rear.

    Break NORTH → cross at the eastern bridge → enter the SOUTH rear.
    Choose a front.The high route is shorter; the fertile route pays more income.
    Use bridges to flank.A cross-bridge is not a third capital route. It lets a breakthrough reach the other front's rear.
    Take the Relay.Each bridge lands on a two-tile Supply Relay. It is a tactical objective, not an income tile.
    Watch the Beacon.Both capitals begin equally far from its opening route.
